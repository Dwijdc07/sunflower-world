03 / VEGETABLES

Vegetables for your channel.

Proposed frozen, dehydrated and selected fresh options for Canadian foodservice, processors and distributors. Product eligibility and logistics are confirmed item by item.

Format, temperature & freight matter

Frozen products require a validated cold chain. Fresh produce needs origin- and commodity-specific import checks, shelf-life planning and an agreed shipping route before a supply commitment.

04 / PACKAGING

Packaging with purpose.

Proposed options for food brands, distributors and general commercial buyers. Material, dimensions, printing and order volume determine sourcing feasibility.

Food contact is application-specific
